About Me

I am an astronomy researcher working on small bodies in the Solar System, with a particular interest in asteroids, comets, and meteoroids. My research interests include physical characterization, cometary activity, orbital evolution, impact phenomena, and time-domain observations. Outside astronomy, I am interested in genetic genealogy (Y: O1b1a1b-MF188438; mtDNA: B4b1a2) and amateur radio (BA7MJD).
